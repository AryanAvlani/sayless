# GPS reel — sources
- Altitude ~20,200 km (~12,550 mi), two orbits a day, 31 operational satellites (as of 2023): GPS.gov Space Segment — https://www.gps.gov/systems/gps/space/ (archive: https://archive.gps.gov/systems/gps/space/)
- Smartphones typically accurate to ~4.9 m (~16 ft) under open sky: GPS.gov Accuracy — https://www.gps.gov/systems/gps/performance/accuracy/
- Receivers only receive (one-way ranging); 4 satellites needed for position + time: GPS.gov / NAVCEN — https://navcen.uscg.gov/
- Signal travel time ≈ 20,200 km ÷ 299,792 km/s ≈ 0.067 s (arithmetic; longer for satellites not overhead)
