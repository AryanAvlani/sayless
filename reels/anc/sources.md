# Noise-cancelling earbuds reel — sources
- Outward-facing microphone detects external sounds, countered with anti-noise, cancelling them before you hear them; inward-facing microphone listens inside your ear and counters remaining sound with anti-noise: Apple Support "Active Noise Cancellation and Transparency modes for AirPods" https://support.apple.com/HT210643
- Anti-noise = equal amplitude, opposite phase (inverted), destructive interference; best for low-frequency noise in transport: IEEE TechNav "Active noise reduction" https://technav.ieee.org/topic/active-noise-reduction/
- Sound is a pressure wave (compression / rarefaction) → "pushes / pulls": IEEE TechNav (superposition of pressure waves)
- "Steady low hums like engines" (periodic, low-frequency noise cancels best): IEEE TechNav (low-frequency attenuation in transport environments)
