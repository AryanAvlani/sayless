# Touchscreen reel — sources
- Grid of transparent ITO rows and columns; cover glass between finger and electrodes; a touch pulls a small charge from the rows/columns: MicroTouch FAQ (touch-panel manufacturer) — https://microtouch.com/faq/ and https://microtouch.com/capacitive-technology/
- Mutual capacitance at each row/column intersection; a touch reduces it; controller scans each intersection: US patents 9715319 and 10078409 (USPTO full text)
- Insulating layer between the row and column ITO layers: Atlantis Press paper on projected capacitive touch screens — https://atlantis-press.com/article/25848126.pdf
- "Up to 240 times a second": Samsung Newsroom Mexico — Galaxy S21 FE / S22 series / A53 have 240 Hz touch response — https://news.samsung.com/mx/descubre-lo-que-son-las-tasas-de-refresco-y-de-respuesta-tactil
- 10+ simultaneous touches: MicroTouch FAQ ("most projected capacitive sensors will recognize between 10 and 80 discrete touch points")
- Ordinary gloves are insulators so capacitive screens ignore them; touchscreen gloves use conductive fibers: SINTEF (research institute) — https://www.sintef.no/en/latest-news/see-me-feel-me-touch-me ; Nelson-Miller (touch-panel maker)
